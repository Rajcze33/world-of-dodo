it is for. The code's own licence is in [LICENSE](LICENSE), the other third-party parts in [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).

## The materials (issue #27): `client/public/assets/mat/`

Made by [`tools/assets/materials.py`](tools/assets/README.md) from 2K texture sets downloaded from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)
and [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) (both CC0 1.0, public domain: no attribution required, given here anyway). The sources are not
in the repository; the links below are the sets' pages and the exact files downloaded. In each row `1024/` and `512/` are the
folders in `client/public/assets/mat/`; `_c` is the colour map (for the surfaces with joints its alpha is the height), `_n`
the normal map; `client/public/assets/mat/index.json` (made by the same script, CC0) lists the keys, their tile sizes and averages. The game
(`client/src/core/matlib.ts`) loads a key's files only when something uses it, in the size of the quality level.
